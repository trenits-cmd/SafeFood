## Introduction

SafeFood is an Android app designed to help you track your food preferences, build safe meal plans, and manage shopping lists. The app uses a 5-star rating system to categorize foods as safe or not safe based on your personal preferences.

## Getting Started

1. Install the SafeFood app on your Android device
2. Launch the app - it will load your food catalog automatically
3. Start rating foods in the Categories or SafeFood tabs
4. Use the features to plan meals and create shopping lists

## Navigation

The app has a bottom navigation bar with the following tabs:
- **Categories** - Browse and manage food categories and items
- **SafeFood** - View your safe and preferred foods
- **Menu** - AI-powered meal planning
- **Shopping** - Manage your shopping list
- **Step-by-Step Planer** - Guided meal planning workflow
- **Picard** - Browse Picard France frozen meal range

The top bar has an **Export CSV** button (download icon) for importing and exporting data.

## Categories Tab

The Categories tab is where you manage your food database.

### Features:
- **Browse Categories** - Expand/collapse food groups to see items
- **Add Category** - Create custom food groups
- **Add Food Item** - Add new foods to any category with notes, nutrient tags, and season
- **Rate Foods** - Use star ratings (0-5) for each food item
- **Mark as Potential Meal** - Click the restaurant icon to mark foods you'd like to use in meal plans
- **Delete Items** - Remove categories or food items (use with caution)
- **Filter by Season** - Filter foods by season (All Year, Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter)
- **Filter by Nutrients** - Filter by Proteins, Fibers, Carbohydrates, Healthy Fats, or Vitamins & Minerals

### How to use:
1. Tap on a category to expand it and see food items
2. Tap the star rating to rate a food (1=Yucky, 5=Yummy; 4-5 are considered safe)
3. Use the "+" button to add new categories or food items
4. Tap the restaurant menu icon to mark/unmark foods as potential meals for AI planning

## SafeFood Tab

The SafeFood tab shows foods rated as safe (4 or 5 stars).

### Features:
- **Safe Foods List** - All foods you've rated as Good (4) or Yummy (5)
- **Recently Rated** - Shows your most recently rated foods
- **Quick Overview** - Easy access to your preferred foods

## Menu Planner (AI)

The AI Menu Planner generates personalized meal plans based on your ratings and preferences.

### Features:
- **AI Generation** - Creates meal plans using your safe foods
- **Planning Goals**:
  - Safe Only - Uses only foods rated 4+ stars
  - Balanced - Mixes safe foods with considerations
  - Mix & Match - More flexible planning
- **Custom Instructions** - Add specific requests for the AI
- **Days Count** - Plan for 1-7 days
- **Use Potential Meals Only** - Generate plans using only foods marked as potential meals
- **Picard Suggestions** - Shows Picard meals that match your criteria (when available)
- **Save Plans** - Generated plans are saved automatically
- **Add to Shopping List** - Add all ingredients from a meal plan day to your shopping list

### How to use:
1. Mark some foods as "Potential Meal" in Categories/SafeFood tabs (optional)
2. Go to Menu tab and configure your preferences
3. Tap "Generate Menu Plan"
4. Review the generated plan and add ingredients to shopping list as needed

## Shopping List Tab

Manage your grocery shopping efficiently.

### Features:
- **Add Items** - Manually add items to your shopping list
- **Status Management** - Mark items as Needed, In Cart, or Already Have
- **Source Tracking** - See which meal plan or Picard meal items came from
- **Clear Completed** - Remove items marked as "Already Have" or completed
- **CSV Import/Export** - Manage your list via CSV files

### How to use:
1. Add items manually or from meal plans/Picard meals
2. Mark items as "In Cart" when shopping
3. Mark as "Already Have" when purchased
4. Use "Clear Completed" to clean up your list

## Step-by-Step Planner Tab

A guided workflow for planning meals step by step.

### Features:
- **Guided Planning** - Walk through meal planning in logical steps
- **Interactive Workflow** - Make selections at each step
- **Integrated with Your Data** - Uses your rated foods and preferences

## Picard Tab

Browse and import Picard France frozen meal range.

### Features:
- **View Picard Range** - Browse available Picard frozen meals
- **Meal Details** - See name, price, cooking instructions, ingredients, photos, servings
- **Update to Latest Range** - Refresh the meal catalog
- **Add to Shopping List** - Add all ingredients from a Picard meal to your shopping list with one tap
- **Food Planning Integration** - Picard meals appear in the Menu Planner when criteria are met

### How to use:
1. Go to the Picard tab
2. Tap "Update to latest range" to load available meals
3. Browse meals and tap "Add to Shopping List" to add ingredients
4. Picard suggestions will also appear in the Menu Planner tab

## Export CSV (Top Bar)

Import and export your data using CSV files.

### Import Section:
- **Import Foods CSV** - Import food items and categories
- **Import Shopping List CSV** - Import shopping list items

### Export Section:
- **Export Foods CSV** - Download your complete food database
- **Export Shopping List CSV** - Download your shopping list
- **Download Template** - Get CSV template files for easy formatting

### Features:
- **Direct Download** - Files are saved directly to your Downloads folder
- **Clear Separation** - Import and export sections are clearly separated
- **Readable Interface** - Clean, organized layout

### How to use:
1. Tap the download icon in the top bar
2. Use "Download Template" to get properly formatted CSV templates
3. Use "Export" buttons to save your current data
4. Use "Import" buttons to load data from CSV files

## Rating System

SafeFood uses a 5-star rating system:

| Rating | Label | Meaning |
|--------|-------|---------|
| 0 | Unrated | Not yet rated |
| 1 | Yucky | Not safe, avoid |
| 2 | Unsure | Borderline, use with caution |
| 3 | Okay | Neutral |
| 4 | Good | Safe option |
| 5 | Yummy | Safe favorite |

**Safe foods** are those rated 4 or 5 stars and appear in the SafeFood tab.

## Tips & Tricks

- **Rate Regularly** - The app will prompt you to re-rate foods older than 5 days to keep your preferences current
- **Use Potential Meals** - Mark foods as potential meals to get better AI-generated menu plans
- **Combine Features** - Generate AI meal plans, add ingredients to shopping list, and shop efficiently
- **Picard Integration** - Use Picard meals for quick options and add their ingredients to your shopping list
- **CSV Backup** - Regularly export your data as backup
- **Seasonal Filtering** - Use season filters to plan meals appropriate for the time of year
- **Nutrient Tracking** - Tag foods with nutrients to create more balanced meal plans
- **Random Rating** - Use the "Food preference check-in" prompts to quickly update old ratings
